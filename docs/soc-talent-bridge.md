# SOC Talent Bridge Initiative

## Purpose

My vision is to connect students' supervised SOC learning with practitioner feedback, mentorship, lab demonstrations, and appropriate workforce opportunities. The initiative builds on the educational SOC Lab at Commonwealth University–Bloomsburg.

[University article](https://www.commonwealthu.edu/news/building-cybersecurity-workforce-soc-lab-experience-cu-bloomsburg) · [Outreach overview](../assets/outreach/soc-talent-bridge-overview.pdf)

## Engagement to date

I shared the overview with companies and industry professionals. I held an initial meeting with Pennsylvania State Police PA CyberCom, shared selected student CVs as potential workforce candidates, maintained communication, and invited the unit to visit the lab.

These activities are described from my account. No formal partnership agreement, completed visit, agency endorsement, or student hiring outcome is documented here. Student CVs and private correspondence are not part of this repository.

[PA CyberCom public information](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/psp/programs/pa-cybercom)

## Proposed collaboration opportunities

- Practitioner review of a sanitized student investigation or detection report.
- Guest discussions about SOC workflows, professional communication, and career preparation.
- Lab visits and demonstrations coordinated through appropriate university channels.
- Mentoring and introductions to suitable internship or employment opportunities.
- Feedback on curriculum relevance as the independent study develops toward a course.

## Next stage

I plan to continue outreach and use practitioner input to refine the lab and curriculum. The proposed initiative does not imply an operating commercial SOC, an agency data-sharing arrangement, or a guaranteed placement program.
