The selected public samples are adapted from Weeks 12 and 13. Their offline evidence fixtures and suggested rubric were created for this portfolio in October 2026. Those additions are not described as original classroom evidence or a historical grading scheme.

## Progression

Early weeks establish case handling, enrichment, and triage. Middle weeks add endpoint collection, team handoffs, and intelligence reasoning. Later weeks ask students to investigate endpoint behavior, evaluate emulation against telemetry, and produce an integrated final report.

## Intended student outputs

The manual asks for incident summaries, timelines, observables, enrichment findings, ATT&CK mappings, analyst justifications, screenshots, and evidence exports. Students are encouraged to prepare sanitized portfolio materials and explain their decisions in interviews. A student's completion or competency level is not established simply by the presence of these assignments.
