## Summary and classification

State the finding, severity rationale, and what remains uncertain.

## Evidence inventory

List file names, source/collection scope, SHA-256 hashes, and handling notes. Keep originals separate from working copies.

## Timeline

Record time, host, activity, and the evidence row/event reference.

## Analysis

Explain observations, alternative explanations, correlation criteria, and limitations. Distinguish visibility from detection and inference from proof.

## ATT&CK mapping

Give technique ID, name, supporting behavior, source reference, and confidence.

## Recommended actions

State escalation or containment recommendations appropriate to the scenario. Identify approval needed for live changes.

## Handoff and lessons

Identify the next question, missing evidence, and an improvement to collection or detection.
